# DeuLern cubist design assets

Exact pixel extractions from the supplied 964 × 1632 flattened design. No artwork has been regenerated or upscaled.

- `illustrations/`: main hero and staircase compositions.
- `apps/`: all four app illustrations.
- `icons/`: three benefit icons, with paper background.
- `decorations/`: geometric edge panels and accents.
- `details/`: optional close crops of the hero components. These overlap the hero and are not independent original layers.
- `textures/`: small paper/ink samples, not seamless tiles.
- `branding/` and `ui/`: original small raster references. Rebuild text, buttons and simple symbols in HTML/CSS/SVG for crisp production rendering.
- `preview.jpg`: labelled contact sheet.
- `manifest.json`: exact crop coordinates and dimensions.

## Transparency and limitations

The hero, staircase and decorative panels have manually traced transparent OUTER boundaries. Their internal paper texture remains intact. Other files are faithful rectangular crops with backgrounds. The screenshot has no original layers: text printed inside illustrations cannot be separated losslessly, obscured artwork cannot be recovered, and crops do not add resolution. The hero silhouette excludes the navigation language selector. Some app index labels overlap the artwork and remain visible.

Use the original PNG dimensions or smaller for sharpness; large desktop use may need higher-resolution source artwork. Treat all normal page copy as live app text rather than a raster image.
