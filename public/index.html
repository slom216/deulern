The apps

Four tools, one gap each.

Most learners get stuck on one thing at a time. Rather than one app that does everything adequately, each of these does a single job properly.

  • Grammatik mit System

    The rules in an order that builds: cases, tenses, word order, subordinate clauses. Every rule is followed by exercises, so you find out immediately whether it landed.

    • Cases & prepositions
    • Sentence structure
    • Exercises per rule
    Open Grammatik mit System

  • Deutsch Wortschatz

    Words grouped by the situations you meet them in, with the article and plural attached from the start. Reviews come back just before you would have forgotten them.

    • Themed word sets
    • Der / die / das drilled
    • Spaced review
    Open Deutsch Wortschatz

  • Deutsch Verben Meistern

    German verbs are where sentences fall apart. Practise them tense by tense — regular, irregular, separable and reflexive — until the right form arrives without thinking.

    • All main tenses
    • Irregular forms
    • Separable prefixes
    Open Deutsch Verben Meistern

  • Lese Reise

    Short German texts graded by level, from A1 to B1. Unknown words are explained in a glossary, and questions after each text check whether you really understood it.

    • A1, A2 and B1 texts
    • Glossary
    • Comprehension questions
    Open Lese Reise

FAQ

Questions worth asking.

Are the apps really free?

Yes. All four apps are free to use in full. There is no trial, no paid tier and nothing to unlock.

Why are you doing this?

We had a lot of struggles learning German. There are many resources, but most of them are paid for. We believe that learning German should be accessible to everyone, with modern approach and modern design. That's why we offer these apps for free.

Do I need an account?

No. Open an app and start practising straight away — there is no sign-up form and no email required.

Which level are the apps for?

They suit beginners through upper intermediate learners, roughly A1 to B1. Start with grammar if you are new, vocabulary if you already know the basics.

Do they work on a phone?

Yes. Every app runs in the browser and adapts to phone, tablet and desktop. There is nothing to install.

Are these apps created by AI?

Yes. All apps and their content are created by AI and monitored by humans, so there may be errors. Every error we identify will be fixed. In future, as our community grows, we will do peer reviews to make the content even better.

Are the apps open source?

Yes. All applications are open source and available on GitHub. You can find the source code for each app in their respective repositories.

Will there be more applications for learning German?

Yes, this is only the beginning. We have in plan adding comic books for easy reading, sentence construction, article practicing, cases practicing, practacing translations, practicing reading and understanding, etc.
