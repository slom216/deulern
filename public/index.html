FAQ

Questions worth asking.

Are the apps really free?

Yes. All three apps are free to use in full. There is no trial, no paid tier and nothing to unlock.

Do I need an account?

No. Open an app and start practising straight away — there is no sign-up form and no email required.

Which level are the apps for?

They suit beginners through upper intermediate learners, roughly A1 to B1. Start with grammar if you are new, vocabulary if you already know the basics.

Do they work on a phone?

Yes. Every app runs in the browser and adapts to phone, tablet and desktop. There is nothing to install.
